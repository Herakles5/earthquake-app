Recent (24H)

    How to read this map

    Live Earthquakes (Map & List)

    Shows earthquakes (M3.0+) from the last 24 hours. The dashed cyan line connects the two most recent quakes, showing the time elapsed between them.

    Short-Term / Global Predictions

    Uses the time gaps between the most recent 5 earthquakes (Short-term) or all 24h earthquakes (Global) to predict when the next event is statistically expected.

    Long-Term Stats

    Analyzes historical data from the last 7 and 30 days to establish average intervals between events.

    • HEAVY (M5.0+ / M7.0+): Significant seismic events.
    • DEEP (>150km): Deep mantle earthquakes, which can act as precursors to larger shallow events. Indicated by a low-pitched sound and a [DEEP] tag.
    • OVERDUE: Indicates that the statistical average time interval has passed without a new earthquake occurring.

    Volcano Activity

    Provides a live feed and status updates on ongoing activity and ash emissions from major active volcanoes worldwide.

    Tsunami Alerts

    Displays active warnings, threat levels, and monitoring status updates for major ocean basins and regions.
